Compact pill for status, role, or category labels.

```jsx
<Badge tone="success" dot>정상</Badge>
<Badge tone="warning" dot>주의</Badge>
<Badge tone="danger">생산필요</Badge>
<Badge tone="violet">슈퍼어드민</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral" solid>팀원</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral` · `info` · `success` · `warning` · `danger` · `violet`. Soft tint by default; `solid` fills; `dot` adds a leading status dot. Sizes `sm`/`md`.
